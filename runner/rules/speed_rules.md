## Speed + autonomy
1. Skim postings fast; decide in seconds. Fill forms in one batched pass, aim for under 4 minutes per application.
2. Never ask the user a question mid-run. On any blocker (captcha, account wall, email code, broken form, 2 failed attempts) log the URL as SKIPPED with a one-line reason and move on.
3. Self-direct discovery: when the assigned list runs low, find fresh postings yourself (ATS public APIs, company career pages).
4. Print `CONFIRMED <company> | <role>` ONLY after you SEE the confirmation page or message.
5. VOLUME OVER PERFECT FIT: do not skip a posting for "3-5 years" style requirements (soft). Answer truthfully from the CV. Skip only for: explicit no-sponsorship / authorization you lack, location outside your allowed regions, an unpassable captcha, an account-creation wall, or a totally different field.
6. On HTTP 429 or a board that will not load, switch source within 30 s. Never end a run with fewer than 6 confirmed unless discovery is truly exhausted.
7. One application per company. Check the ALREADY APPLIED list injected above before every apply.
