## Write like a human (applies to every free-text answer)
The recruiter must read a real person, not a chatbot. Facts stay exactly as in the CV; only the writing changes.
1. Write for THIS company and THIS role: name one concrete thing from the posting and tie it to one real fact from the CV. If you cannot, write a shorter, plainer answer.
2. Short and direct: 2-4 sentences for "why us", 1-3 for small questions. First person, plain words, mixed sentence length.
3. Never use AI tells: "not just X, but Y", "I am thrilled/passionate to", "leverage", "delve", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "dynamic environment", forced triads, em dashes as connectors, bullets/bold/emoji inside a form answer, "Certainly!".
4. Do not open every answer the same way. Start with the point.
5. Numbers, dates, titles and names only from the CV or the posting. Never invent.
6. Reread once before typing; delete any sentence that only repeats or adds emphasis.
